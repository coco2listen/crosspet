## 格式规则

```
You are drawing desktop-pet sprites for an existing chibi character. The attached image is the ONLY design reference.

FORMAT RULES (every image):
- Real transparent background (generate with transparent_background: true). Nothing else: no floor, no ground shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame. Keep complete, clean edges.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no speech bubbles, no symbols (no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons floating in the air). The app draws all effects itself. Phone screens only glow; never draw anything readable on them.
- Exactly one character, full body, standing, centered, facing the viewer like the reference. Head near the top and feet near the bottom; she fills about 90% of the image height, the same scale and position as the reference.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette (hair, jacket, sleeves, skirt and shoes included).
- Same art style as the reference: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, chibi about 2.5 heads tall, big head, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger. One image per file, saved under the exact filename given.
- Change ONLY the expression, the arms/hands, and small props. Hair, accessories, outfit, colors and body features stay exactly as in the reference.
- Every prop she holds TOUCHES her hand; nothing floats in the air. Props are small and simple so they still read at about 200 px.
```

## 角色锁定

```
CHARACTER LOCK — Grok, the anime-girl personification of Grok (the AI from xAI), a cheeky street-style space girl.
Personality: quick-witted, sassy and playful; answers everything fast with a joke on top; fact-checks everyone (even her own boss) and loves a good comeback; a rebellious little sister, never mean or cruel. Her default face is a confident smirk.
Keep exactly:
- Messy, shoulder-length SILVER-WHITE hair with one BLACK streak on her left side (viewer's right); one springy ahoge on top.
- Bright RED eyes with sharp highlights, a slight smirk, a tiny fang visible when she grins.
- A "black hole" hair clip on her left side (viewer's right): a small glossy black disc crossed by one white diagonal slash.
- An OVERSIZED black cropped flight (bomber) jacket with white piping, worn open; the sleeves are too long and cover half of her hands. On her left shoulder a round patch with the same black-disc-and-white-slash mark. A tiny white rocket charm hangs from the jacket zipper.
- Under the jacket: a white high-neck cropped top. A black pleated mini skirt with a thin silver chain belt. Chunky black-and-white high-top sneakers with white laces.
- Signature prop: a slim black smartphone (screen only glows, nothing drawn on it). Keep it in her hand or jacket pocket in most poses unless the pose says otherwise.
Palette: black and white with silver, plus small accents of RED (eyes, a red lace tip, the rocket's flame). No other strong colors.
How she shows emotion: a smirk, a raised eyebrow, a wink with the tongue out, hands shoved in her jacket pockets, flipping the jacket collar up when annoyed, holding her phone out at the viewer when she has "fact-checked" something.
```
